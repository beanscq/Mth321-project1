\section{Reference Solution and Validation}
The numerical experiments use a cross-checked reference trajectory for error comparison. It is used to measure convergence, stability failure, and endpoint error.

The numerical error has three main sources in this study: time discretisation, the nonlinear algebraic solve, and floating-point round-off. The Robertson equations are treated as the prescribed mathematical model, so modelling error relative to real chemistry is outside the scope. The algebraic and round-off components should remain below the time-discretisation error when measuring formal convergence. Step refinement, Newton-residual studies, and finite-difference checks are used to compare these error sources.

The common comparison grid contains $t=0$ and 200 logarithmically spaced output points from $10^{-8}$ to $40$, for 201 points in total. Internal steps are method-specific. The reference trajectory is computed with Radau using
\[
\mathrm{rtol}=10^{-10},\qquad
\mathrm{atol}=(10^{-14},10^{-20},10^{-14}),
\]
and repeated after reducing both tolerances by a factor of 100:
\[
\mathrm{rtol}=10^{-12},\qquad
\mathrm{atol}=(10^{-16},10^{-22},10^{-16}).
\]
BDF and LSODA provide independent numerical cross-checks of the reference trajectory~\cite{scipy}. The Jacobian identity separately checks the algebraic structure of the model.

The reference computations agree closely across tolerance refinement and solver choice. Table~\ref{tab:ref-endpoints} lists the endpoints of all four runs, and Table~\ref{tab:ref-agreement} summarises their agreement. The repeated Radau runs agree to $10.75875$ digits (minimum over components), BDF to $10.70288$ digits, and LSODA to $10.98101$ digits; the largest componentwise cross-check difference is $2.089\times10^{-12}$, and no pairwise difference on the common grid exceeds $3.8\times10^{-12}$. These differences measure agreement among the numerical references rather than a bound on the unknown true error.

The reference trajectory satisfies the expected non-negativity and conservation conditions on the comparison grid: its maximum invariant defect is $2.442491\times10^{-15}$ and all concentrations remain non-negative. Because the endpoint is reproduced to $10.76$ digits by tolerance repetition and to at least $10.70$ digits by two structurally different solvers, $y_2(40)$ supports ten significant digits. We therefore report
\[
y_2(40)=9.185535\times10^{-6},
\]
conservative by four digits relative to the confirmed agreement.
